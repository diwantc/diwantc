DiwanTC adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, especially to beginners.
- Criticize ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or sharing others' private information.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainer at:
**[conduct@diwantc.com](mailto:conduct@diwantc.com)**

Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may remove content, or warn,
suspend or ban participants, as described in the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
